# Target Audience

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## Primary
Philander Smith graduating students with varied aspirations (first job, graduate school, service, entrepreneurship, undecided) and diverse accessibility, schedule, and technology needs.

## Secondary
Potential program coordinators, alumni/mentors, career advisors, and judges viewing a prototype. Secondary actors are illustrative only; none have confirmed access or institutional authorization.

## Inclusion
Support part-time work schedules, intermittent connectivity, students who feel uncertain, nontraditional paths, motion sensitivity and assistive technologies. Never present a career-only definition of success.
