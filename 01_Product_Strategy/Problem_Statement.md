# Problem Statement

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## Problem
Graduation is a clear date, but readiness for the world beyond campus is diffuse. Students may not know which next steps deserve attention, which resources exist, or how to translate anxiety into action.

## Causes to test
Fragmented information; disconnected programs; competing academic deadlines; low clarity around financial decisions; unequal access to networks; desire for spaces to reflect and celebrate. These are design hypotheses, **not measured findings about Philander Smith students**.

## What success means
A student can name their next step, identify sources of support, complete useful tangible artifacts, and see growth across the final 100 days.
