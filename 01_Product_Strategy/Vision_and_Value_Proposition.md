# Vision and Value Proposition

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

**Vision:** Every graduating student leaves campus equipped to navigate transition with confidence, connection, and purpose.

**Mission:** Make the final 100 days an inviting, structured, self-directed passage from reflection to action.

**Value for students:** clarity about next steps, meaningful preparation rather than busywork, an affirming record of growth. **Potential value for campus:** repeatable content framework for successive cohorts, conditional on campus adoption.

**Differentiator:** the spatial chapter map creates a legible emotional story; milestone artifacts make every node useful; the final threshold ties preparation to legacy.
