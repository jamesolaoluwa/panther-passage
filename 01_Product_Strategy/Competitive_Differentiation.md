# Competitive Differentiation

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## Against existing categories
A checklist organizes tasks but lacks ritual and motivation. A career portal may organize professional documents but not financial, wellbeing or legacy transitions. Event listings provide opportunities without a personal progression arc. Casual games create legible progression but rarely require meaningful offline outcomes.

**Panther Passage synthesis:** collegiate pathway visual language + tangible life preparation + personal timeline + celebratory final threshold.

## Avoid copying
No Candy Crush icons, board layouts, audio, art, branded interactions or IP. Borrow only the high-level principle of sequential discoverable nodes.
