# Product Overview

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## In one sentence
Panther Passage is a student-directed preparation journey through the last 100 days before graduation. Students complete practical tasks, gain visibility into resources and events, document reflections, and celebrate a personal launch plan.

## Five experience pillars
- **Orient:** see day countdown and chapter map; choose intended post-graduation direction.
- **Prepare:** career, money, wellbeing, moving, and community skills with actionable outputs.
- **Connect:** discover curated guidance and simulated opportunities; identify people to reach out to.
- **Reflect:** privately preserve lessons, milestones, and legacy.
- **Launch:** assemble a 30-day plan and complete a celebratory threshold moment.

## Not in prototype
Real transcripts, degree audits, official financial aid, institutional SSO, actual event registration, real mentor matching, personalized career advice, university data feeds.

## Student agency
Activities are suggestions, never assessment of worth. No fear-based countdown or shame for late starts. Accessibility mode includes a linear list view.
