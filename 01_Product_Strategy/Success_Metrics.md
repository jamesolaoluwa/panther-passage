# Success Metrics

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## Prototype measures (instrument locally for testing, not claimed outcomes)
- Onboarding completion: target 90% in a supervised 5-person usability pilot (hypothesis).
- Locate next recommended milestone: target within 15 seconds for 4/5 pilot participants.
- Complete first milestone: target within 5 minutes after onboarding, including feedback.
- Navigate spatial map with keyboard: 100% nodes reachable.
- Persist and reload a task: 100% of automated checks.
- Explain distinction between days left and tasks done: 4/5 participants after demo.

## Campus pilot metrics later
Opt-in usage, completion rate by milestone, student-reported clarity/self-efficacy before/after, resource click-through, accessibility issues. Small samples cannot substantiate causal career success. Establish consent and retention before collecting.
