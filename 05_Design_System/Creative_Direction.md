# Creative Direction

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## Art direction
High-craft contemporary HBCU-inspired editorial storytelling: confident expressive serif headlines, roomy whitespace, intentional asymmetry, confident plum/ink fields, antique-gold path line, tactile illustration, and an architectural threshold. Design is **inspired by a campus passage** but the provisional vectors are not drawings of an authentic specific building.

## Avoid
Generic SaaS templates, rainbow gradients, stock photos of anonymous students, pseudo-official campus photography, excess greenery, copied Candy Crush candy/art, cartoonish progress animations, meaningless sparkles and inaccessible microtext.

## Screen mood
Welcome: invitation. Home: clarity. Map: anticipation. Detail: agency. Resources: usefulness. Reflections: intimacy. Final destination: dignified celebration.
