# Iconography

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

Icons should use a consistent 2px outline at 24px grid; route metaphor appears in larger vector artworks. Suggested Lucide icons under their own license: map, house, compass, book-open, check, lock-keyhole, calendar-days, user-round, award, arrow-right. UI icons describe tasks, not decoration. Original SVG arch/crests are supplied as provisional illustrative assets; don't mix an unknown university crest with mock awards.
