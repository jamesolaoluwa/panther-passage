# Brand Guidelines

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## Voice
Warm, poetic in introductions; clear and literal in actions. Never condescending or guarantee outcomes. Headlines can be evocative but every CTA should tell the student precisely what happens. Example: `The next step is yours.` paired with `Start Resume Checklist`.

## Logo
An approved Panther Passage logo/wordmark was referenced in the conversation but original binary is unavailable in this package. `09_Assets/Icons/panther-passage-mark-provisional.svg` is a **temporary original arch symbol, not the approved logo**. Do not substitute it in final approved brand surfaces. Obtain exact approved files and clear-space/license specifications before delivery. University marks are not included.

## Signature motifs
Arch as passage; dotted gold route; numbered medallions; five chapter crests; paper grain at subtle opacity; minimal plant shapes. All should remain readable without imagery.

## Tone guidelines
Say 'Your first step' rather than 'You are behind'. Say 'Demo RSVP saved' rather than 'Registration confirmed'. Say 'Preparation progress' rather than 'Graduation status'.
