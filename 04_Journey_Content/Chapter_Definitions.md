# Chapter Definitions

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## P1: Find Your Footing
**Window:** 100–81 days left, advisory only. **Promise:** Reflect on who you are and locate your starting point. **Theme:** Identity & intention. **Chapter crest:** Find Your Footing Crest. **Visual:** `09_Assets/Chapters/chapter-01.svg`. **Actions:** Name Your North Star, Take Your Readiness Snapshot, Collect Your Story.

## P2: Build Your Toolkit
**Window:** 80–61 days left, advisory only. **Promise:** Translate ambitions into tangible career and life readiness skills. **Theme:** Readiness & resources. **Chapter crest:** Build Your Toolkit Crest. **Visual:** `09_Assets/Chapters/chapter-02.svg`. **Actions:** Build Your Resume, Introduce Your Future Self, Make a Connection.

## P3: Make Your Moves
**Window:** 60–41 days left, advisory only. **Promise:** Turn preparation into practical steps, connections, and choices. **Theme:** Action & connection. **Chapter crest:** Make Your Moves Crest. **Visual:** `09_Assets/Chapters/chapter-03.svg`. **Actions:** Explore Your Options, Practice the Interview, Find a Support Circle.

## P4: Ready for What’s Next
**Window:** 40–21 days left, advisory only. **Promise:** Develop financial, wellness, and transition plans. **Theme:** Confidence & continuity. **Chapter crest:** Ready for What’s Next Crest. **Visual:** `09_Assets/Chapters/chapter-04.svg`. **Actions:** Map Your Money, Plan Your Wellbeing, Explore a City or Community.

## P5: Carry It Forward
**Window:** 20–0 days left, advisory only. **Promise:** Celebrate growth, document legacy, and leave with a next-step plan. **Theme:** Legacy & launch. **Chapter crest:** Carry It Forward Crest. **Visual:** `09_Assets/Chapters/chapter-05.svg`. **Actions:** Create Your 30-Day Launch Plan, Leave a Legacy Note, Cross the Threshold.
