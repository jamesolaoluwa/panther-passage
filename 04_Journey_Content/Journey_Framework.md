# Journey Framework

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

## Proposed chapter sequence
### P1 — Find Your Footing (days 100–81)
Reflect on who you are and locate your starting point. Emotional emphasis: Identity & intention. Milestones: M01, M02, M03.
### P2 — Build Your Toolkit (days 80–61)
Translate ambitions into tangible career and life readiness skills. Emotional emphasis: Readiness & resources. Milestones: M04, M05, M06.
### P3 — Make Your Moves (days 60–41)
Turn preparation into practical steps, connections, and choices. Emotional emphasis: Action & connection. Milestones: M07, M08, M09.
### P4 — Ready for What’s Next (days 40–21)
Develop financial, wellness, and transition plans. Emotional emphasis: Confidence & continuity. Milestones: M10, M11, M12.
### P5 — Carry It Forward (days 20–0)
Celebrate growth, document legacy, and leave with a next-step plan. Emotional emphasis: Legacy & launch. Milestones: M13, M14, M15.

## Time vs preparation
Days remaining is calendar arithmetic; preparation percent is based on actual completion. Calendar phase is an editorial cue only: a late starter can complete earlier nodes in any reasonable order after dependencies; never punish timing.

## Unlock philosophy
At most one preceding required milestone blocks the next relevant required activity; optional activities can enrich the experience without locking the finale. Final destination is available only after ceremony-required work.
