## What each pathway screen contains

Each preview is a real route. It shows a pathway overview, three catalog milestones, one activity with its own prompts, a progress line, and a next-chapter summary that appears after the three preview checks. Checking a box and choosing “Save preview progress” left Jordan Ellis’s stored task checks empty.

- Continue My Education: M07 Explore Your Options, M12 Explore a City or Community, M13 Create Your 30-Day Launch Plan. Activity asks for programs, a real requirement, and an application step.
- Start My Career: M04 Name Your Professional Direction, M05 Introduce Your Future Self, M06 Make a Connection. The page states that this path does not include a résumé or an interview.
- Start a Business: M07, M10 Map Your Money, M13. Activity asks for an offer, costs, and a money decision. The approved pathway name on the screen is Start a Business.
- Independent Living & Financial Readiness: M10, M12, M13. The money prompts are housing, monthly costs, and a money boundary.
- Purpose, Wellness & Personal Growth: M03 Collect Your Story, M11 Plan Your Wellbeing, M14 Leave a Legacy Note. Activity asks about pressure, rest, and one practice.
