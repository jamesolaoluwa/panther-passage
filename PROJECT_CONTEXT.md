# Context and origin

## The challenge
The last 100 days of college can be an inflection point: graduating students may need support with careers, financial independence, entrepreneurship, personal wellbeing, relocation, identity, networks, and community connection. This framing comes from the challenge brief supplied in the project conversation; it is not presented as survey evidence.

## Panther Passage response
Turn a looming graduation deadline into a student-owned set of meaningful and manageable steps. Present the experience as an editorial pathway with a university-inspired threshold/arch, tactile milestones, chapter crests, and an affirming graduation destination.

## Brand decisions from project conversation
Creative direction: Expressive Collegiate Editorial; Recoleta Black-inspired signature display typography; high-impact, non-generic type; path progression inspired by Candy Crush without cartoon mimicry; fewer campus trees and less greenery; white background for relevant approved presentation reference; exact welcome sentence: 'Plan your next steps, discover resources, and move confidently toward graduation and beyond.'

## Evidence boundaries
No student interviews, study outcomes, live campus partnerships, university enrollment/access, financial data, or approved hex values were provided for this package.
