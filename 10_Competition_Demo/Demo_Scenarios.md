Select new; zero milestones; demonstrate the first three-task moment and unlock.

Seven milestones seeded completed (M01–M07); browse next available career or graduate-school prep step; demonstrate resources and simulated RSVP.

Every fictional student uses same 2027-05-15 mock graduation date. Because actual date may differ, UI countdown must derive from current local date rather than hardcoding 100/60/5 days for these scenarios. For narrative certainty, label scenarios by progress, not days remaining.
