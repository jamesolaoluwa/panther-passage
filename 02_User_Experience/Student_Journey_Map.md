# Student Journey Map

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

| Moment | Without concept (hypothesis) | With Panther Passage | Feeling to support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 days | Many abstract worries | Choose intention, see full arc | Relief |
| 80 days | Career pressure increases | Build concrete resume and narrative | Agency |
| 60 days | Outreach may be intimidating | Script respectful connection step | Courage |
| 40 days | Life logistics become salient | Budget, wellbeing, relocation planning | Confidence |
| Final days | Uncertain what follows | Review accomplishments, legacy note, 30-day launch plan | Pride and possibility |

This is a proposed service journey, not an observed journey study.
