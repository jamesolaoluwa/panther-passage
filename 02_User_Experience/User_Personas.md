# User Personas

> **Status:** Proposed implementation guidance, not an institutional commitment. Panther Passage is a competition concept for Philander Smith University. All sample people, events and services are fictional/simulated unless explicitly labeled externally verified.

### Jordan — starting out (fictional)
Career curious; wants a clear small first step; needs high contrast, low commitment and quick onboarding. Struggles with many unprioritized tasks. Success: one saved intention and visible next node.

### Avery — moving forward (fictional)
Exploring graduate school; has some completed milestones; balances applications and time. Success: identify available task, filter relevant resource, update a personal plan.

### Morgan — on the threshold (fictional)
Entrepreneurship-minded, nearly complete; values symbolic closure and community contributions. Success: leave a legacy note and view a dignified completion ritual.

Personas are synthetic scenarios, **not drawn from validated student interviews**. Validate goals and obstacles with prospective users.
